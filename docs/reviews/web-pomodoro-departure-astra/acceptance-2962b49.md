## Why the final implementation meets the business contract

Each real producer registers a distinct latest field operation. Completion's existing next-preset producer uses the same path. Success removes only its matching draft; Retry ignores an in-flight latest operation and becomes eligible after that operation's own failure, so a predecessor Promise cannot clear a queued successor. Existing uncertainty tokens stay in the accepted async preference engine. Matching retry success clears unload/host protection only after physical persistence; failed or changed uncertainty preserves it. Source-only repair does not fabricate a draft or discard a sibling.

All externally supplied guard methods now check live account epoch against the captured decision, including isBlocking. An old or disposed permission refuses while a fresh current permission guards the surviving device draft. Export uses live checks before setup and at the click boundary. Current full-memory exports retain exactly the accepted six-value JSON schema, avoid timer/account payloads, remain usable under total storage denial and preserve the guard on both success and failure. Beforeunload performs no storage or export operation.

Conflict-only recovery now deletes only the matching conflict fields and delegates their reread to the existing hook. With native theme conflict and sound quota failure, theme adopts external violet while sound bell remains unsaved and guarded. Sound can subsequently Retry and persist, export a complete snapshot without clearing the guard, or be explicitly discarded through the restored ordinary button or the current departure capability. The latter two paths perform zero preference writes, do not reread already recovered theme and leave timer bytes unchanged. The real Chrome mixed-conflict test independently keeps actual navigation blocked until the remaining sound operation succeeds.

The plugin exposes an optional structurally typed registration callback; it imports no app/Settings runtime. The app-owned Pomodoro adapter uses the accepted reusable host coordinator and stable callback. Feature-disabled fallback, empty/wildcard routes, rail order and metadata remain intact. Generic sign-out names and Settings compatibility aliases refer to one current delegate; App's captured auth/scope checks remain unchanged. The pre-existing extracted coordinator retains first-intent arbitration, numeric/relative routing, cancellation, focus and unmount restoration rather than adding a second state machine.

The accepted timer/history and device-preference boundaries remain intact: running alone is clean; preference changes/recovery do not reset or settle timer state; completion still writes one history record/emits once, even when next preset fails and is later retried. Full CSS now keeps recovery actions inside their notice and reachable in the actual Shell. The old geometry-only visual PASS is not used as a substitute for the corrected hit-tested and manually inspected final evidence.
