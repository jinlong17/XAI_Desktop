### 3.2 Proposed measurement hypothesis, not an accepted algorithm

Preserve the original allowance of at most one browser body transition during cycle traversal; never confuse it with the prohibited body landing after a Clock action. 2. **Clock stops.** Preserve stable, exactly aligned focused and moved-on own-box/ring captures with the existing coordinate semantics (edge difference <0.01 px and identical clips, scroll and hover). Observe selected/unselected styles and active/inactive timezone entries separately. Compare decoded pixels in that control's own box/outline band; exclude the next control's influence. Record computed outline and band/interior/own deltas. No own-region change, masked outline or absent computed outline is a Clock business FAIL when measurement is valid. A transformed Clock target is **not** newly tolerated by the outside-stop exception: report invalidity and return for method review.
3. **Transformed outside stops.** Proposed route: retain stable native full-viewport frames for each actual focused/unfocused state, measured boxes and outline regions, and crop both frames to the **same viewport union** of those actual boxes/ring extents. This prevents the current unequal-size/position clip refusal without removing the Tab stop. No screenshot scaling or geometric registration warp. The union is calculated from both real measured states; same device scale, viewport, browser zoom, all ancestor/window scroll positions and pointer/hover chain are required. Stability must be shown for repeated captures **within each state's region**; full-frame timestamps and local crops are preserved, so unrelated Clock ticking is not silently counted as a focus signal.
4. **Causality and obstruction.** Observe layout anchors/ancestors before/after to distinguish the known target's focus transform from page movement. Exclude the union of the next stop's actual changed box/ring regions in both states, not only a fixed rectangle. If exclusion leaves no attributable target pixels, declare the measurement inconclusive/invalid, not PASS. Check actual hit/paint coverage: an occluded target cannot become visible because an overlay or next stop changes pixels. Unknown occluders, overflow clipping or unstable layout prevent a valid pixel verdict; a stable fully occluded control is an obstruction FAIL/record according to its ownership, never green. 5. **No cosmetic workaround.** Do not change product transforms, outlines, selection, transitions, overlays, sizes, pet, timers or hover CSS; do not wait indefinitely, increase geometric tolerance, lower pixel thresholds, hide the next target, privately focus nodes, bypass centre hits or click through another control.
